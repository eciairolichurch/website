About ECI

The Evangelical Church of India (ECI) was founded by the Oriental Missionary Society, beginning its work in India in 1941. ECI's vision is to see a transformed India where every person has the opportunity to hear and respond to the Gospel. The church is committed to serving communities through evangelism, church planting, and holistic community development.

Goal of ECI:

MARCHING TOWARDS THE CENTENARY VISION TO PLANT 100 THOUSAND CHURCHES
